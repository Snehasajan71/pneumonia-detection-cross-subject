Clinical Trade-Offs & Key Findings

  • Chest CT Scans provide 3D axial spatial resolution enabling detection of early Ground-Glass Opacities (GGO) prior to X-Ray visibility.
  • Chest X-Rays serve as the optimal first-line low-cost screening modality with zero 3D radiation overhead.
  • Cross-Subject Validation eliminates patient anatomical memorization across both imaging modalities.
