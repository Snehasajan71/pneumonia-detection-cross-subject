Why Cross-Subject Validation Matters

Standard K-Fold randomly assigns images across splits. When a patient has multiple X-ray scans, their distinct anatomical features appear in both training and test sets—causing Data Leakage and inflated validation scores.

Random K-Fold (Baseline) Same Patient images split across Train & Test $\rightarrow$ Leakage Risk!
Stratified GroupKFold (Our Project) Strict Patient ID grouping $\rightarrow$ Zero Overlap ($train \\cap val = \\emptyset$)
